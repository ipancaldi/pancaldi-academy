8-week timeline

From founder interview to a complete neighbourhood brand.

Eight connected chapters build one fictional business. Each client checkpoint changes the work without changing the underlying facts, audience or ambition.

Week 1

Interview, Research & Strategy

Understand the founder before designing the brand.

Client moment
  • Interview fictional founder Maya Bennett about the business model, values, budget, offer and ambitions for an Islington opening.
Studio work
  • Research Islington coffee culture, local competitors, neighbourhood behaviours, primary audience and strategic white space.
Output
  • Interview notes, market and neighbourhood audit, audience profiles, positioning statement and strategic brief.
Week 2

Naming, Logo & Creative Territories

Give the business a name and several possible futures.

Client moment
  • Test naming criteria and present three distinct routes to Maya; respond to her concerns about warmth, credibility and memorability.
Studio work
  • Create name candidates, logo explorations and several creative territories with different strategic and visual ideas.
Output
  • Naming shortlist, trademark-screening notes, logo sketch sheets and three territory boards with rationale.
Week 3

The Identity System

Choose one territory and make it repeatable.

Client moment
  • Confirm the chosen direction with Maya and resolve how it must flex between café hospitality, packaged coffee and apparel.
Studio work
  • Define typography, colour, layout, imagery, tone of voice, logo behaviour and system rules.
Output
  • Core identity, type and colour specifications, image direction, messaging principles and concise brand guidelines.
Week 4

Corporate Identity & Physical World

Make the brand believable from storefront to coffee cup.

Client moment
  • Maya prioritises what must be ready for opening day and asks the system to work at both flagship and everyday scales.
Studio work
  • Apply the system to corporate materials, signage, posters, takeaway cups and mugs, clothing and merchandise.
Output
  • A bento-style presentation of storefront signs, menus, stationery, posters, cups, T-shirts, caps, bottles, bags and mugs.
Week 5

Coffee Product Packaging

Turn the identity into a clear, scalable product family.

Client moment
  • Maya shares the fictional launch range: three single-origin coffees, one house blend and a seasonal gift set.
Studio work
  • Build packaging architecture, product hierarchy, information panels, labels and a system for future varieties.
Output
  • Coffee bags, labels, shipping box, gift set, product cards and a packaging system sheet.
Week 6

Neighbourhood Launch Campaign

Create local anticipation across streets and screens.

Client moment
  • Maya sets an opening date, a modest local media budget and a goal: earn attention without feeling like a chain.
Studio work
  • Develop one campaign idea across physical and digital touchpoints with channel-specific pacing and copy.
Output
  • Posters, window vinyl, street handout, social launch sequence, email, digital ads and opening-week activation.
Week 7

Website & Digital Storefront

Bring the café, coffee and clothing into one simple experience.

Client moment
  • Maya clarifies the key journeys: find the café, understand the brand, buy beans and discover the clothing line.
Studio work
  • Plan information architecture, responsive layouts, product storytelling and a fictional location page for 62 Bellwood Rise, Islington.
Output
  • Simple responsive website with homepage, café, coffee shop, clothing and merchandise, product detail and location pages.
Week 8

Refinement & Portfolio Case Study

Edit eight weeks of work into one convincing story.

Client moment
  • Present the complete launch to Maya, answer final objections and agree the details that make the brand feel ready.
Studio work
  • Refine consistency, art-direct mockups and write the story from research and strategy through identity, packaging, campaign and website.
Output
  • One complete portfolio case study, a presentation deck, final brand archive and a concise reflection on the client process.
